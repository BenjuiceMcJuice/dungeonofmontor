Portrait phone layout: the room on top, touch controls underneath. The game renders at 320x192 (20x12 tiles of 16px) and scales up in whole pixels. Tap a number to find it in the list.

    A run through this room

    1. ENTER

      Doors seal

      You come in from B1 on the left. It's a combat chamber, so the right door locks and a Dust Rat, a bat and a Slate Slug wake up. AGI decides how long they take to react.

    2. FIGHT

      Aim, then the dice

      Your sword swing has to touch the rat. When it does, the game rolls the same d20 the Crawl uses and shows the tier as a floating number.

    3. HAZARDS

      Use the room

      The puddle makes anything standing in it WET. Hit a wet rat with the Cattle Prod and CONDUCTANCE chains across the water.

    4. SEARCH

      Dig the junk

      With the room clear, hold interact on a pile, pick Quick Rummage, Thorough or Deep Clean, and roll d20 + PER. The terminal might be in the big mound.

    5. LEAVE

      Pick an exit

      The right door unlocks. Or climb the ladder to the hatch and go up to A2. The room remembers what you killed, opened and searched.

    The hidden dice

    Skill decides whether you connect. The d20 decides how well. These are the Crawl's own numbers: sword d6, STR 12 (+1), the +4 bonus for a hit that physically lands, against a Dust Rat with 8 HP and DEF 5.

    Enemies roll the same way when they touch you. A dodge-roll through an attack is a guaranteed avoid. It costs stamina, which END pays for.

    The Crawl's maze generator is unchanged. B2 connects left to B1 (door), right to B3 (door) and up to A2 (ladder and hatch). A connection downwards would be a drop hole in the floor.

    Weapons change your moves

    Sword: a medium arc and a downward stab in the air. Dagger: a fast three-hit combo, and you can attack mid-roll. Spear: a long poke, and bouncing on enemies with a down-attack. Mace: a slow overhead that stuns on stagger. Axe: a huge arc that steps you forward. Fists: grab a rat and throw it.
